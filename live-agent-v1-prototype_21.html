The self-serve guide didn't fix it

Camila is a regional sales manager on the road in Madrid, 30 minutes from a client presentation, with a VPN that won't connect. She has already described the problem, sent a screenshot, and been given the matching knowledge-base guide. V1 picks up here — at the moment self-serve runs out and live help has to take over. What comes before (stages 1–2: free-text start, identity, language detection) is the ideal experience and sits outside V1 scope; it plays as history so the conversation is already underway.
